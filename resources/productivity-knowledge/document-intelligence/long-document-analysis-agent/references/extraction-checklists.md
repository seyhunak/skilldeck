# Extraction Checklists by Document Type

Use the checklist that matches the document. Every extracted item records its page and section. Mark items as "not addressed" when the document is silent; never fill in a market-standard default.

## Commercial contracts

| Area | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Parties and roles | Legal names, roles, affiliates covered |
| Term and renewal | Start date, initial term, auto-renewal mechanics, non-renewal notice window |
| Payment | Fees, invoicing timing, payment period, late interest, price increase mechanism |
| Obligations | Per party: what, standard of performance, service levels and credits |
| Termination | For convenience (who, notice), for cause (cure period), on insolvency, change of control; effects of termination |
| Liability | Cap amount and basis, carve-outs from the cap, excluded loss types |
| Indemnities | Who indemnifies whom, for what, procedure, caps |
| Warranties | What is warranted, duration, remedies, disclaimers |
| IP | Ownership of deliverables, licences granted, background IP |
| Confidentiality | Scope, exceptions, duration after termination |
| Data protection | Roles (controller/processor where stated), security commitments, breach notification timing, sub-processors |
| Assignment and subcontracting | Consent requirements |
| Disputes | Governing law, forum, escalation steps, arbitration |
| Notices | Method, addresses, deemed receipt rules |
| Boilerplate traps | Entire agreement, order of precedence between documents, amendment formalities |

## Annual reports and financial filings

- Reporting period, currency, units (thousands vs millions), and accounting framework as stated.
- Headline figures: revenue, operating profit, net income, cash from operations, net debt — with the page of the primary statement, not the highlights page.
- Segment figures and any restatements or changes in accounting policy.
- Guidance and forward-looking statements, quoted with their qualifiers.
- Risk factors: list, and note new risk factors versus the prior year when available.
- Auditor's opinion type and any emphasis-of-matter or key audit matters.
- Related-party transactions, contingent liabilities, subsequent events.

Check: figures quoted in the narrative match the primary statements. Report any mismatch with both page references.

## RFPs and tenders

- Issuer, reference number, submission deadline (with time zone), clarification-question deadline.
- Mandatory (pass/fail) requirements versus scored requirements.
- Evaluation criteria and weights.
- Required format, page limits, required forms and certifications.
- Commercial terms that are non-negotiable.
- Build a compliance matrix: | Req ID | Requirement | Section | Mandatory? | Response owner |

## Regulations and standards

- Scope and applicability conditions (who and what is covered, thresholds).
- Effective dates and transition periods.
- Obligations phrased with "shall" / "must" versus guidance ("should", "may").
- Definitions section and cross-references to other instruments.
- Penalties or consequences as stated.

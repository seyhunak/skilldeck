## 9. Synthesis approach

- E2 wrong intervention or exposure
- E3 wrong comparator
- E4 wrong outcome
- E5 wrong study design
- E6 not primary research
- E7 outside date range
- E8 duplicate publication of same data
- E9 full text not retrievable (report separately, not as a judgement)
